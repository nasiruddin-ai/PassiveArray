# Keyword plan: 100 searches to win

The content plan for the Passive Array blog. Every keyword below is a real
phrase people type, not a guess.

## How this list was made

1. **Harvested from autocomplete.** Google web search and YouTube search
   autocomplete were queried for 34 seed topics, each expanded with letter and
   modifier suffixes. That produced 4,678 unique real phrases. Autocomplete is
   a record of what people actually type, not a model of it.
2. **Filtered to our territory.** Phrases were kept only where Passive Array
   has a tool, an extension feature, or a formula it can explain honestly.
   Navigational searches for competitors, and searches in other languages,
   were dropped.
3. **Scored and bucketed.** Ranked by whether the phrase appears in both
   engines, whether it is a question (blog-friendly), whether it implies a
   tool, and length. Then grouped into the six blog categories.

### Why there are no search volume numbers here

Nobody outside Google has YouTube search volume, and web volume for these
phrases is only available from paid tools that model it. Publishing a made-up
"8,100 / month" next to each row would look authoritative and mean nothing.
That is the opposite of how this site works. Priority below is based on
evidence we actually have: whether the phrase appears in both autocomplete
engines, how many close variants exist, and whether we can answer it better
than the pages currently ranking.

## Rules for every article on this list

1. **Answer the search in the first hundred words.** People arriving from a
   question want the answer, not a preamble.
2. **Show the formula.** Every number on this site is reproducible. If an
   article cannot show its working, it is not ready.
3. **Ranges, not fake precision.** Where the honest answer is a range, give
   the range and explain what moves you inside it.
4. **Never invent a statistic.** No fabricated search volumes, no invented
   survey results, no made-up averages. If we do not have it, we say so.
5. **Link to the tool that does the job**, and to the two or three related
   articles in the same category.

## Refreshing this list

Re-run the harvest any time. The script lives in the session notes and uses
only free autocomplete endpoints:

```
https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&q=<seed>
https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&ds=yt&hl=en&q=<seed>
```

Query each seed alone, then with each letter a to z appended. Dedupe, drop
anything with no tool or article behind it, and bucket into the six
categories in `blog/build-blog.js`.
